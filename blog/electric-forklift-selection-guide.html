Electric Forklift Selection Guide: Capacity, Lift Height and Battery Choices

Published: October 20, 2026 · Xi'an Chenma Materials Co., Ltd.

Direct answer: choose an electric forklift by matching the heaviest load, required lift height, operating format, and the battery and motor fields documented for the exact model. Verified Chenma full-electric examples range from 500 kg to 2000 kg, while semi-electric examples range from 400 kg to 2000 kg. These are model-specific records, not a universal category specification.

For a purchase team, start with the record covering load and height, confirm seated, stand-on or manual operation, then check battery, motor, dimensions and fork data against the site. CHENMA LIFTING can prepare a model-specific quotation, subject to final configuration confirmation.

2. Capacity: use the exact rated field

Capacity is the first filter. F07 states 2000 kg / 3 m, F06 states 500 kg / 210 cm, and F02-1 and F04 each have documented 800 kg options with different formats and dimensions. Lower-load examples include 400, 500, 800 and 1000 kg. S08 is configuration-sensitive: it lists 1000 / 1500 / 2000 kg and 1600–3500 mm lifting heights. Confirm the selected combination rather than assuming every height carries every load.

Record the heaviest package, normal package, load-center information and support format. If the product record does not answer an engineering question, mark it for confirmation rather than inventing a safety or residual-capacity value.

3. Lift height: match the task and configuration

Read lift height from the exact product field. F02-1, F02-2 and F04 state 2 m; F06 states 210 cm; F07 states 300 cm. S01 and S03 state 1.6 m, S05 1.7 m, S06 1.3 m, and S12 lists 1.3 m / 1.6 m. S08 records 1600 to 3500 mm. Ask where the load must be placed, what clearance exists, and whether the quoted configuration supports that height. The records do not provide a universal residual-capacity curve; do not infer one from maximum lift.

Review the CMAE-F07 2000 kg / 3 m page and CMAE-F06 500 kg / 2.1 m page for model-specific context.

4. Full-electric versus semi-electric operation

Treat FE and SE as operating-format categories, not promises about every control or drive function. F02 records identify seated operation; F04 stand-on operation; S01 button plus remote control; S02 manual operation; and S03 wired plus wireless remote control. Choose only after matching the exact record to the load, height, operating position and site. Ask for travel, lift and control behavior in the quotation.

5. Battery and motor choices

Battery is a configuration checkpoint. FE examples list 48V lead-acid systems: F02-1 52AH, F02-2 45AH, F04/F06 32A and F07 32A. S13, S01 and S02 list 48V / 15AH lithium; S03 12V lead-acid DC; S05 24V / 20A lead-acid; S06 48V / 15A lead-acid; and S08 DC 12V without chemistry. F06 lists 2000 W drive / 2500 W hoist, F07 5000 W / 4000 W, and F02-1/F02-2 1500 W / 1200 W. Do not turn power into an unverified load, duty-cycle or battery-life claim. For background, see the lithium vs lead-acid battery guide.

6. Dimensions, forks and site fit

Capacity is not enough if the machine cannot fit the aisle or load. F02-1/F02-2 state 185×100×220 cm without forks. F04 lists adjustable 20–90 cm width and 92 cm forks for its 800 kg record; its 500 kg record lists 20–80 cm and 80 cm. F06 states L230 cm / W78 cm / H158 cm and a 95 cm radius; F07 states L337 cm / W120 cm / H215 cm. SE fit fields include S01 1.17 m forks / 56 cm outer width, S03 33 cm / 60 cm, S08 1070 mm / 680 mm and S12 65×10 cm forks with 60 cm outer width. Verify site measurements.

7. RFQ checklist

  1. Exact load range and heaviest stated load.
  2. Required lift height and configuration option.
  3. Operating format and documented control method.
  4. Available electrical supply and battery field to quote.
  5. Fork length, width, load dimensions and support format.
  6. Aisle, turning and doorway measurements.
  7. Expected work pattern without assuming unlisted runtime.
  8. Documentation or site requirements for engineering confirmation.

This checklist keeps a comparison focused on evidence. CHENMA LIFTING can respond against the selected product record instead of a broad category label.

Frequently asked questions

What is the documented load range in this Chenma selection guide?

The referenced FE and SE examples span 400 kg to 2000 kg, while the selected 10 FE and 7 SE records include model-specific values. The value must be checked against the exact model and configuration.

Which verified model is listed at 2000 kg and 3 m?

CMAE-F07 Heavy Duty is listed at 2000 kg load capacity and 300 cm maximum lift height. Its product record also lists a 48V / 32A lead-acid battery, 5000 W drive motor and 4000 W hoist motor.

Which model has several rated-load and lift-height options?

CMSE-S08 lists rated loads of 1000 / 1500 / 2000 kg and lifting heights of 1600 / 2000 / 2500 / 3000 / 3500 mm. The quotation should confirm the selected load-height combination.

Are all electric forklifts in the range lithium powered?

No. The verified records include lead-acid, lithium and unspecified battery chemistry fields. Examples include F02-1 lead-acid, S13 lithium, S03 12V lead-acid DC and S08 DC 12V without a chemistry field.

How should I compare full-electric and semi-electric models?

Compare the exact documented operating format, load, lift height, battery, motor, fork and dimension fields. Do not assume that a category name alone defines travel, lifting or control behavior.

What should I ask for when the product record lacks a parameter?

List the missing parameter in the RFQ and ask the engineering team to confirm it for the exact configuration. Do not substitute a generic safety factor, residual-capacity curve, battery-life figure, certification claim or operating promise.
