Electric Forklift Drive Technology: Brushless vs. Standard Motor

CMAE-R10 (Brushless Motor, 800kg / 2m)

CMAE-F02 (Standard Drive, 800kg / 2m)

When comparing electric forklifts, the drive motor technology is just as important as battery capacity. Chenma offers two 800kg-class models with distinct approaches: the CMAE-R10 with a brushless motor and heavy-duty battery bank, and the CMAE-F02 with a standard motor and a compact battery pack.

Direct Answer: The Selection Rule

Choose the CMAE-R10 when you need longer runtime and reduced maintenance, thanks to its brushless motor and 4-battery configuration. Choose the CMAE-F02 for a lighter, more compact machine where occasional duty cycles are sufficient.

What the Numbers Mean for You

  • Brushless Motor Efficiency (CMAE-R10): Brushless motors have no carbon brushes to wear out, reducing maintenance frequency and improving energy efficiency over the machine's life.
  • Runtime & Heavy Duty (CMAE-R10): The 4x 48V 75AH battery bank supports extended multi-shift operation. The 1100 kg net weight provides better stability under full load.
  • Compact & Cost-Effective (CMAE-F02): The single 48V 52AH battery and 450 kg weight make it easy to maneuver in narrow aisles and budget-friendly to acquire and run.
  • Common Ground: Both models share solid rubber tires, 800kg capacity, and 2m lift height - suitable for standard pallet work.

Long-Term Cost Logic

Total Cost of Ownership = Purchase Price + Energy + Maintenance - Resale Value

  • Maintenance: Brushless motors eliminate brush replacement, a recurring cost on standard motors.
  • Battery Replacement: Lead-acid batteries are replaced every 3-5 years depending on charge cycles; the R10's larger bank costs more upfront but spreads the cost over longer runtime.
  • Duty Cycle Fit: Buying more battery than you need (or less) is the most common cost mistake - match the machine to your daily shift hours.
