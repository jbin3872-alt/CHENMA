How to Choose an Electric Forklift

An electric forklift is a 5-10 year investment, and choosing the wrong one costs more than the machine itself - in downtime, repairs, and operator risk. This guide walks through the four decision dimensions, battery and motor trade-offs, and an 8-point checklist to ask before you buy. All examples use real Chenma models: CMAE-F07 (2000kg, 5000W), CMAE-F02-1 (800kg, 1500W) and CMAE-R10 (800kg, 1500W brushless).

Direct Answer: The 4 Decision Dimensions

Choose an electric forklift in four steps: first match load capacity to your heaviest regular load, then lift height to your racking, then battery type to your shift pattern, and finally power and site conditions (motor rating, floor, aisle width). Work through them in this order and the right model narrows itself down.

Battery Selection: Lead-Acid vs Lithium

Lead-Acid

  • Cost: lower upfront purchase price
  • Life: 4-6 years typical with proper watering
  • Charging: 6-8 hours plus cool-down; opportunity charging not recommended
  • Maintenance: regular electrolyte top-up and terminal cleaning
  • Best for: single-shift operations, budget-first buyers
  • Chenma trucks use 48V lead-acid packs - proven, recyclable, widely serviceable

Lithium

  • Cost: significantly higher initial investment
  • Life: 8-10 years with minimal capacity fade
  • Charging: 1-2 hours, opportunity charging during breaks
  • Maintenance: maintenance-free, no watering
  • Best for: multi-shift, high-throughput, cold-storage operations
  • Evaluate total cost of ownership over the machine's life, not just the sticker price

Brushless vs Brushed Motor

Factor Brushed Motor Brushless Motor (e.g. CMAE-R10 1500W)
Efficiency Lower - friction and heat losses at brushes Higher - less heat, more power to the wheels
Maintenance Brushes wear out and need periodic replacement No brushes to replace - lower service cost
Reliability Good, but brush wear limits service life Excellent for long, continuous duty cycles
Best use Light intermittent duty, budget models Frequent operation where uptime matters

8-Point Checklist Before You Buy

  1. Rated capacity: is it above your heaviest regular load with a safety margin?
  2. Lift height: does it reach your top racking level without the load shifting?
  3. Motor power: 1500W for light-medium duty, 5000W for heavy industrial loads (CMAE-F07)
  4. Battery type and size: does the pack cover a full shift (e.g. 48V 52AH / 75AH x4)?
  5. Charging setup: do you have a ventilated charging bay and spare battery strategy?
  6. Aisle and door width: does the truck's turning radius fit your site layout?
  7. Certification: is the model CE certified and supplied with documentation?
  8. After-sales support: spare parts availability, warranty terms, and service network

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose the right load capacity for an electric forklift?

Match the forklift's rated capacity to your heaviest typical load plus a safety margin. For light warehouse duty up to 800 kg, the Chenma CMAE-F02-1 or CMAE-R10 fits; for heavy industrial loads up to 2000 kg, choose the CMAE-F07 (5000W motor, 3m lift).

What is the difference between lead-acid and lithium forklift batteries?

Lead-acid batteries cost less upfront and are proven and recyclable, but need regular watering and 6-8 hour charge cycles with cool-down time. Lithium batteries charge faster, last longer, need no maintenance, but cost more initially. Chenma electric forklifts use maintenance-friendly 48V lead-acid packs.

What is the power rating of the Chenma CMAE-F07 electric forklift?

The Chenma CMAE-F07 Heavy Duty electric forklift is powered by a 5000W motor with a 48V lead-acid battery, offering a 2000 kg load capacity and 3 m lift height.

Why choose a brushless motor forklift like the CMAE-R10?

The CMAE-R10 uses a 1500W brushless motor that delivers higher efficiency, less heat, and lower maintenance than brushed motors - no brushes to replace - while carrying 800 kg with a 2 m lift from a 48V 75AH lead-acid pack x4.
