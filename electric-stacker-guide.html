Full-Electric vs. Semi-Electric Stacker: Which One to Choose?

CMAE-S13 (Full-Electric)

CMSE-S01 (Semi-Electric)

Choosing the right stacker is critical for warehouse efficiency. While both models handle 1-ton loads, the operational difference between Full-Electric (CMAE-S13) and Semi-Electric (CMSE-S01) can significantly impact your productivity.

Direct Answer: The Selection Rule

Use Full-Electric (CMAE-S13) if you transport goods over long distances frequently. Use Semi-Electric (CMSE-S01) for static lifting or occasional short-distance moves where budget is a primary concern.

Model Comparison

Feature CMAE-S13 (Full) CMSE-S01 (Semi)

Key Decision Factors

  • Labor Fatigue: The CMAE-S13 eliminates the need to push 1-ton loads manually, preventing operator strain.
  • Maneuverability: Both feature compact designs. However, the CMAE-S13's motorized wheels provide better precision in tight corners under full load.
  • Charging & Runtime: Both use high-efficiency lithium batteries, ensuring ~6 hours of intermittent work time.
