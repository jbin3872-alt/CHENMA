# Electric Forklift Selection Guide: Capacity, Lift Height and Battery Choices

## Direct answer: choose from the load, lift, operating format and documented power system

Choose an electric forklift by matching four facts in order: the heaviest load you need to handle, the required lift height, the operating format that fits your workflow, and the battery and motor specification recorded for the candidate model. Do not select from a family name alone. In the verified Chenma product records, full-electric examples range from 500 kg to 2000 kg, while semi-electric examples range from 400 kg to 2000 kg. The lift heights in the examples below range from 1.3 m to 3.5 m, but those values belong to specific models and configurations, not to every forklift in the category.

For a purchase team, start with the record covering load and height, confirm seated, stand-on or manual operation, then check battery, motor, dimensions and fork data against the site. CHENMA LIFTING can prepare a model-specific quotation, subject to final configuration confirmation.

## 2. Capacity: compare the rated field, not a marketing label

Capacity is the first filter. Use the load field for the exact model and configuration: F07 states 2000 kg / 3 m, F06 states 500 kg / 210 cm, and F02-1 and F04 each have documented 800 kg options with different formats and dimensions. Lower-load examples include 400, 500, 800 and 1000 kg. S08 is configuration-sensitive: it lists 1000 / 1500 / 2000 kg and 1600–3500 mm lifting heights. Confirm the selected combination rather than assuming every height carries every load. Record the heaviest package, normal package, load center information and support format; mark unanswered engineering fields for confirmation.

## 3. Lift height: separate mast height from the task height

Lift height should be read from the exact product field. F02-1, F02-2 and F04 examples state 2 m; F06 states a maximum lift height of 210 cm; F07 states 300 cm. S01 and S03 state 1.6 m, S05 states 1.7 m, S06 states 1.3 m, and S12 lists 1.3 m / 1.6 m. S08 is the widest documented height range, from 1600 to 3500 mm.

Ask where the load must be placed, what clearance exists, and whether the quoted configuration supports that height. Include storage height, load dimensions and machine dimensions in the RFQ. The records do not provide a universal residual-capacity curve; do not infer one from maximum lift.

For a model-specific starting point, review the [CMAE-F07 2000 kg / 3 m page](https://www.chenmalifting.com/products/cmae-f07-heavy-duty-2000kg-3m.html) and the [CMAE-F06 500 kg / 2.1 m page](https://www.chenmalifting.com/products/cmae-f06-precision-forklift-500kg-21m.html).

## 4. Full-electric versus semi-electric operation

“Full-electric” and “semi-electric” should be treated as operating-format categories in this shortlist, not as promises about every control or drive function. The verified records show useful differences in documented fields. F02 records identify seated operation; F04 identifies stand-on operation; S01 lists button plus remote control; S02 lists manual operation; and S03 lists wired plus wireless remote control.

Choose a full-electric example when the specific record matches the required load, height, operating position and dimensions. Choose a semi-electric example when its documented operation and configuration fit the material flow, especially when a compact or configuration-specific stacker is more appropriate. Ask for the exact travel, lift and control behavior in the quotation; do not assume that all FE models share one control system or that all SE models share one manual/electric split.

## 5. Battery and motor choices: use the product record

Treat the battery field as a configuration checkpoint. FE examples use 48V lead-acid records: F02-1 52AH, F02-2 45AH, F04/F06 32A and F07 32A. S13, S01 and S02 list 48V / 15AH lithium; S03 lists 12V lead-acid DC, S05 24V / 20A lead-acid, S06 48V / 15A lead-acid, and S08 DC 12V without chemistry. Motor fields are also model-specific: F06 lists 2000 W drive / 2500 W hoist, F07 5000 W / 4000 W, and F02-1/F02-2 1500 W / 1200 W. Do not turn power into an unverified load, duty-cycle or battery-life claim; request confirmation for the selected configuration.

For chemistry background only, see the existing [lithium vs lead-acid battery guide](https://www.chenmalifting.com/lithium-vs-lead-acid-battery.html), then return to the exact battery field on the product page.

## 6. Dimensions, forks and site fit

Capacity is not enough if the machine cannot fit the aisle or load. F02-1/F02-2 state 185×100×220 cm without forks. F04 lists adjustable 20–90 cm width and 92 cm forks for its 800 kg record; its 500 kg record lists 20–80 cm and 80 cm. F06 states L230 cm / W78 cm / H158 cm and a 95 cm turning radius; F07 states L337 cm / W120 cm / H215 cm. SE fit fields include S01 1.17 m forks / 56 cm outer width, S03 33 cm / 60 cm, S08 1070 mm / 680 mm and S12 65×10 cm forks with 60 cm outer width. Verify the site measurement.

## 7. RFQ checklist for a reliable comparison

Send the supplier a short, model-specific brief containing:

1. The exact load range and heaviest stated load.
2. Required lift height and whether the requested option is one of several configurations.
3. Operating format: seated, stand-on, manual, button, wired remote or wireless remote when documented.
4. Available electrical supply and the battery voltage/capacity or chemistry you want quoted.
5. Fork length, fork width, load dimensions and pallet/support format.
6. Aisle, turning and doorway measurements, using the product’s stated dimensions as a starting point.
7. Expected work pattern and charging/handling routine, without assuming an unlisted runtime.
8. Any documentation or site requirements that must be confirmed by the engineering team.

This checklist keeps a comparison focused on evidence. CHENMA LIFTING can then respond against the selected product record instead of a broad category label.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the documented load range in this Chenma selection guide?

The referenced FE and SE examples span 400 kg to 2000 kg, while the selected 10 FE and 7 SE records include the model-specific values shown above. The value must be checked against the exact model and configuration.

### Which verified model is listed at 2000 kg and 3 m?

CMAE-F07 Heavy Duty is listed at 2000 kg load capacity and 300 cm maximum lift height. Its product record also lists a 48V / 32A lead-acid battery, 5000 W drive motor and 4000 W hoist motor.

### Which model has several rated-load and lift-height options?

CMSE-S08 lists rated loads of 1000 / 1500 / 2000 kg and lifting heights of 1600 / 2000 / 2500 / 3000 / 3500 mm. The quotation should confirm the selected load-height combination.

### Are all electric forklifts in the range lithium powered?

No. The verified records include lead-acid, lithium and unspecified battery chemistry fields. Examples include F02-1 lead-acid, S13 lithium, S03 12V lead-acid DC and S08 DC 12V without a chemistry field.

### How should I compare full-electric and semi-electric models?

Compare the exact documented operating format, load, lift height, battery, motor, fork and dimension fields. Do not assume that a category name alone defines travel, lifting or control behavior.

### What should I ask for when the product record lacks a parameter?

List the missing parameter in the RFQ and ask the engineering team to confirm it for the exact configuration. Do not substitute a generic safety factor, residual-capacity curve, battery-life figure, certification claim or operating promise.
