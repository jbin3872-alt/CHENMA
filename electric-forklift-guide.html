How to Maintain Your Electric Forklift

CMAE-F07 (Heavy Duty 2000kg / 3m)

CMAE-F02 (Compact 800kg / 2m)

A well-maintained electric forklift can last 3-5 years longer and operate at maximum safety. Whether you run the heavy-duty CMAE-F07 (2000kg) or the compact CMAE-F02 (800kg), these maintenance routines will protect your investment and your operators.

Direct Answer: The Maintenance Rule

Spend 15 minutes a day on pre-shift inspections and schedule a professional service every 500 hours of operation. This prevents 80% of unexpected breakdowns.

Daily Pre-Shift Checklist

  • Battery & Charger: Check the 48V charge level. Lead-acid batteries (CMAE-F07 / F02) should be charged after 80% discharge, never left at 0%.
  • Hydraulic System: Inspect for oil leaks around the mast, forks, and tilt cylinders. Top up hydraulic oil if the level is low.
  • Brakes: Test both the drum brakes and the parking brake before loading. The CMAE-F07 features a handbrake + footbrake system.
  • Wheels & Tires: Check the solid rubber tires (Front 400-10 / Rear 400-8 on the F02) for wear, cuts, or embedded debris.
  • Forks: Verify the forged monoblock forks are not bent or cracked, and that the fork locks engage properly.

Weekly Service Tasks

  • Mast Lubrication: Grease the 10# C-channel steel mast rails to ensure smooth lifting.
  • Electrical Connections: Tighten and clean battery terminals to prevent corrosion and voltage drops.
  • Battery Watering: For lead-acid batteries, top up with distilled water to just above the plates (NEVER tap water).
