How to Choose the Right Aerial Work Lifting Platform

CMLM-E09 (Self-Propelled Scissor Lift)

CMLM-E03 (High-Altitude Lift)

When selecting an aerial work platform, the two most critical factors are working height and mobility. Chenma offers two distinct solutions: the self-propelled CMLM-E09 Scissor Lift for heavy-load indoor/outdoor projects, and the versatile CMLM-E03 Galvanized Lift for ultra-high access.

Direct Answer: The Selection Rule

Choose the CMLM-E09 if you need a mobile platform with large capacity (450kg) and self-driving capability up to 10m. Choose the CMLM-E03 for lightweight workers reaching extreme heights of 4-20m on a budget.

Safety Checklist for Aerial Work

  • Ground Assessment: Ensure the ground is level and capable of supporting the machine's weight plus load.
  • Perimeter Safety: Set up warning signs and barriers around the working area to prevent unauthorized access.
  • Weather Check: Do NOT operate the CMLM-E03 or any high-altitude lift in winds exceeding 12.5 m/s (Beaufort 6).
  • Harness Mandatory: Operators must wear full-body safety harnesses anchored to designated points.
